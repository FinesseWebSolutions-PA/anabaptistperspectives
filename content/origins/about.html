The people and the vision behind Origins.

Why We Made This

They were hunted, imprisoned, exiled, put to death, and still the movement grew. Anabaptist Origins is a documentary series that tells the first hundred years of that story, the birth of Anabaptism. Filmed across six European countries, it lets you see firsthand the council chambers that condemned them, the prisons that held them, the rivers and squares where they died.

Behind the Mic

Series Host

Reagan Schrock

Reagan Schrock co-founded Anabaptist Perspectives in 2017. Reagan graduated from Sharon Mennonite Bible Institute and completed a B.A. in Biblical Studies with Lancaster Bible College in 2021. Reagan also helped start an Anabaptist ministry that focuses on missions in unreached regions around the world. As a result, Reagan is involved in travel, teaching, and leadership with this organization (name withheld for security reasons). Reagan resides in Tennessee with his wife, Patricia, and two daughters.

Teacher & Historian

Stephen Russell

Stephen Russell is an instructor of theology and church history at Faith Builders Educational Programs. He earned a degree in European Studies at George Mason University (1986-1988), and an M.A. in Church History and Theology at Wheaton Graduate School (1989). His voluntary service experiences include two summers in Germany (1978, 1982), Choice Books (1981), and a halfway house for parolees (1980). He taught in Christian schools for five years and worked at Choice Books for seventeen years. During a seven month stay in Israel (2006), he wrote a book on nonresistance, Overcoming Evil God’s Way. The opportunity to teach at Faith Builders has been the culmination of a longtime dream.

Teacher & Historian

Dean Taylor

Over 30 years ago, Dean Taylor and his wife, Tania, walked away from military life after a life-altering encounter with the teachings of Jesus in Scripture. That pivotal season included witnessing the fall of the Berlin Wall in 1989—a moment that stirred both political and spiritual reflection. As they read the Gospels and studied the writings of the early church, they were drawn deeply into the historic Christian witness of nonresistance and discipleship. This blend of Scripture and church history opened a path that eventually led them into the Anabaptist communities.

    Since that time, Dean and his family have been immersed in education and missions. They served on the island of Lesvos, Greece, where they have worked with refugees through I58 and Christian Aid Ministries (CAM). Dean was ordained as a bishop for the mission in Greece by Mine Road Beachy Amish Church in Pennsylvania.
     
    Today, Dean and his family live in Sugarcreek, Ohio, where they are members of Crosspointe Mennonite Church and serve as part of the ministry team. Dean is president of the Zollikon Institute, an Anabaptist educational institution committed to nurturing a new generation of church leaders and missionaries. He carries a deep passion for Anabaptist education and missions, holding to the conviction that the local church must remain central to both.
     
    Dean is completing a ThD in historical theology. He continues to study history—not only as an academic pursuit but as a source of wisdom, conviction, and guidance for the church today.
